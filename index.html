OpenCode Go Strategy & Model Selection Guide

Optimal model operation strategy under the official $10/mo OpenCode Go subscription:

1. Daily Coding Driver: $60/mo Tier (x6.0 Multiplier)

GLM-5.3-Flash (Score 41.8) costs just $0.040 per 1M effective tokens with a generous 6,320 requests / 5h limit. For ultra-cheap high volume, MiMo-V2.6-Flash (Score 37.9, $0.029, 30,100 req/5h) is unbeatable.

2. The $15/mo Tier Trap (GPT-6 Luna Eliminated)

While GPT 6 Luna looks cheap at $0.20 raw, on Go it is capped at $15 (x1.5 multiplier), raising effective cost to $0.133. It is thoroughly outclassed by GLM-5.3-Flash ($0.040, score 41.8) and drops off the frontier.

3. Heavy Reasoning & Agentic Tasks

For complex refactoring and debugging, MiMo-V2.6-Pro (Score 46.3, effective $0.362) strikes the perfect balance, consuming less than 1/5th the quota of Grok 4.7 ($2.000, 169 req/5h).

4. 5-Hour Rolling Limit Management

$15 tier models like Kimi K3 (110 req/5h) or Grok 4.7 (169 req/5h) quickly trigger 5h lockouts during intensive coding sessions. Route heavy work to $60 tier models whenever possible.
